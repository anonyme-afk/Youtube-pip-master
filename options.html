Example: Alt+P  ·  Ctrl+Shift+P  ·  Ctrl+Q

How it works
On YouTube, a PiP button is injected directly into the player controls bar. On any other website, a floating button appears when you hover over a video. Use the keyboard shortcut or the button to detach the video into a floating window.
